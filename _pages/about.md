## Welcome!

Hello, and welcome to my website!

I am an **Industrial Microbiologist, researcher, and science educator** with expertise in **marine microbial ecology, coral microbiomes, biotechnology, molecular biology, and environmental conservation**.

My career has combined scientific research, laboratory management, project coordination, teaching, and mentoring. I am passionate about understanding the interactions between microorganisms and marine ecosystems, while also inspiring students to develop curiosity, critical thinking, and a love for science.

Currently, I work as a **Science Teacher**, where I strive to connect classroom learning with real-world scientific research through inquiry-based and hands-on learning experiences.

Previously, I worked at the **Smithsonian Tropical Research Institute (STRI)** in Panama, where I coordinated laboratory operations, managed research projects, mentored students, and conducted molecular and microbiological research focused on coral resilience and reef restoration.
