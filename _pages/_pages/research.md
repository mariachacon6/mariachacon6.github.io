## Research Interests

My research focuses on marine microbial ecology, coral microbiomes, reef resilience, biotechnology, and environmental conservation.

## Coral Microbiomes and Reef Resilience

I investigate the relationships between corals and their associated microorganisms, with particular interest in understanding how microbial communities contribute to coral health, resilience, and reef restoration.

## Marine Microbial Ecology

My work includes molecular and microbiological approaches to study microbial diversity in coral reefs and deep-sea environments.

## The Role of Bacteria in Coral Resilience

I led the research project **“The Role of Bacteria in Coral Resilience,”** supported by USD 59,378 in federal research funding. The project explored the potential contribution of coral-associated bacteria to coral resilience and restoration.

## Coral Research in Panama and the Galápagos

I contributed to multi-year research examining coral resilience across seasonal and geographic gradients in the Eastern Tropical Pacific and Caribbean, including research sites in Panama and the Galápagos Islands.

## Deep-Sea Microbial Diversity

My previous research included the study of fungi associated with deep-sea corals and the molecular annotation of marine fungal genes.

## Environmental Biotechnology

I have also worked on phosphate-solubilizing bacteria, biochar-based bioinoculants, soil microbiology, and sustainable agricultural biotechnology.
